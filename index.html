About me

I'm a graphic designer based in Unaizah, Saudi Arabia, with more than nine years of professional experience in branding, social media, marketing campaigns, print, motion graphics and video editing.

I've developed full brand identities, reports, publications and digital content for associations, academies and agencies. I'm comfortable running a design team as well as producing the work myself.
